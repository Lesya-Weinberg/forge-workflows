# Plan profile — Nano

Smallest planning touch. For single-file features and small scripts.

**Planners that fire:** `product-manager` (◐ brief only), `backlog-planner` (◐ tasks only).
**Skipped:** business-analyst, ux-*, solution-architect, security, data-*, config-manager.

**Depth:**
- product-manager writes a short brief (problem, outcome, constraints) — not a full PRD — and a
  flat task list instead of formal stories.
- backlog-planner converts the brief into 0–2 backlog items (often just tasks) and applies a
  light Definition of Ready (acceptance criteria stated; no missing inputs).

**Gate:** default `auto-proceed` is appropriate; one digest at the end of planning is enough.

When nano scope grows past a single file or reveals a real design question, upgrade the tier and
re-route — do not stretch the nano profile.
